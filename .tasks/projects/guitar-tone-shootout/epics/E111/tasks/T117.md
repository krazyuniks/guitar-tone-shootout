## Objective

`model_downloader.py` exists with complete download+checksum logic, but `sync_service.py` never imports or calls it. The downloader must be integrated into the sync loop so model files are actually downloaded after model staging.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] After model staging in `sync_models()`, `ModelDownloader.download_models_for_pack(pack_id)` is called
- [ ] Downloaded files appear at `source_downloads/t3k/{model_id}/{filename}`
- [ ] `sync_service.py` imports and instantiates `ModelDownloader`
- [ ] Download failures do not crash the sync loop (logged and continued)
